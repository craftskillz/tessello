# Notes

Jot down short notes. They stay in your Modules folder, on your computer.

## Write a note

Open **Notes** from the Launcher, type in **Write a note…** and click **Add note**. The newest note comes first.

Click **Delete** next to a note to remove it. It is deleted at once.

## On the Home

The Notes Widget shows your newest note and how many notes you have.

- Click the Widget to open Notes.
- Click **+** to open Notes ready for a new note.

## Your data

Your notes are saved as soon as you add or delete one. Uninstalling Notes deletes them.
