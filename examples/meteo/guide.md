# Weather

The current weather of the cities you follow, from [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com).

## Follow a city

Open **Weather** from the Launcher. Pick a city in the list and click **Follow**. Paris, New York and Tokyo are followed at first.

Click a city to see its weather. Click **×** next to a city to stop following it.

## On the Home

The Weather Widget shows the temperature in each city you follow. Click a city to open Weather on it.

## Network

Weather asks the Open-Meteo servers (`api.open-meteo.com`) for the weather, and nothing else. If they cannot be reached, it says "The weather can't be loaded right now": try again later.

## Your data

The cities you follow are saved as soon as you change them. Uninstalling Weather forgets them.
