# Watch Later

**Keep the YouTube videos you want to watch later: paste or drop a link, find it again.**

Every change is saved the moment you make it. There is no Save button.

## Adding a video

Copy the link of a YouTube video, then paste it in the field at the top of the page and press **Enter**, or paste it anywhere on the page with **Ctrl+V** (**Cmd+V** on a Mac). You can also drag a link onto the page, from a browser tab, the address bar or another page, and drop it where it says **Drop to add**. Links to a video, a Short, a live stream or an embedded player are all recognized, and so is the moment a link starts at.

Several links at once, one per line, are all added: the page then says how many were added, how many were already there and how many were not videos. A video is never added twice: if it is already in your list, even from another link or at another moment, the page shows its card instead.

The video appears at once with its thumbnail, then with its title and its channel, read from YouTube. If YouTube cannot be reached, the link stands in for the title: click **Retry** to read it again.

## Tags and rows

Tags sort your videos by theme. In the details of a video, type a tag in **Add a tag** and press **Enter**; the tags you already use are suggested as you type. Click the **×** of a tag to take it off. Tags ignore case and extra spaces: `music` and ` Music ` are the same tag, written as you first wrote it.

To tag videos as you add them, choose tags in **Tags for new videos**, under the field: every video you add next gets them, until you close the page.

The page shows one row per tag, in alphabetical order, then a **No tag** row for the videos without one. A video with two tags is in both rows. Each row says how many videos it holds and scrolls sideways: with a trackpad, with **Shift** and the mouse wheel, with a finger, or with the **‹ ›** buttons. The page itself scrolls from one tag to the next.

## Watching

Click the thumbnail or the title of a video to open it on YouTube, in a new tab. In each row, the newest videos come first, and each card says when you added it.

Once you have watched a video, click **Watched** on its card; click it again to put the video back among those to watch. **Hide watched**, at the top of the page, is on at first: a video you mark as watched then leaves the page, and **Undo**, in the message that follows, brings it back. Uncheck **Hide watched** to see every video, the watched ones dimmed and marked. The page remembers your choice.

## Notes and deleting

Click **Details** on a card, or anywhere on the card but its title and thumbnail, to see its channel, when you added it and whether you watched it. Write in **Note** why you kept the video, up to 2,000 characters: the note is saved when you leave the field or close the details, and a ✎ on the card tells you it has one. **Delete** removes the video from your list once you confirm it.

## When something goes wrong

- **Your videos could not be read**: Tessello could not read what the Module saved. Nothing can be added until they are read, so that an empty list never replaces yours. Click **Try again**.
- **Your last change could not be saved**: the change is still on screen, but it will be lost when you leave. If the message says the storage is full, the Module holds more than Tessello allows it.
- **This video is already in your list**: the video you pasted or dropped is there already; its card is highlighted a moment.
- **This is not a YouTube video**: what you pasted or dropped is not a link to a YouTube video, so nothing was added.
- **A link instead of a title, with Retry**: YouTube could not be reached, or the video is private. The video is kept all the same; click **Retry** once you are back online.

## Your data

Your videos are saved in your Tessello space, on your computer, and nowhere else. To show a video, the Module contacts YouTube: `www.youtube.com`, once, to read its title and channel, and `i.ytimg.com` to show its thumbnail. Uninstalling the Module deletes your videos.
