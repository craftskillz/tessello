# Skills Library

**Keep the agent skills you have judged, in one place.**

Every change is saved the moment you make it. There is no Save button.

## Adding a skill by hand

Click **Add manually**, type the name of the skill, and optionally its URL and a description, then click **Add**. The skill appears at the top of the **Library**, to watch, with three stars, in the first quadrant. Its name is a link to its URL, when it has one.

## The tabs

- **Library** lists your skills, the newest first, with their ring, their stars and their quadrant.
- **Radar** and **Editions** are coming soon.

The page remembers the tab you left it on.
